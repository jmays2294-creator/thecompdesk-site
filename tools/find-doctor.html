Heads up: NY law changed

Under the 2024 Comprehensive Reform of the Reimbursement Program (CRRP), every licensed physician in New York may now treat workers'-compensation patients without separate WCB authorization for most services. You are not limited to "WCB-authorized" doctors — this map highlights providers experienced with WC cases, but your treating physician can be any licensed NY doctor. Certain specialty exams (IMEs, permanency evaluations) still require specific WCB credentials.
