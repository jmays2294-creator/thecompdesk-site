01 · Inherited Rate Header

Set the AWW once. Every tile inherits it.

The header pins your TT rate, the date-of-injury Max and Min, and a source label showing where it came from — global, case, or AWW override.

If AWW falls below the statutory minimum, the calculator quietly collapses every rate to AWW and tells you right here. No more chasing the floor by hand.

Designation per period. Math is automatic.

02 · TT · RE · TR · TP · NCLT · NME

Tap a chip for each period. TT runs the inherited ⅔ rate. RE drops reported earnings off AWW before the ⅔ math. TR takes a percent of the uncapped ⅔ × AWW — so 87.5% TR doesn't quietly undershoot when AWW is high.

TP, NCLT, and NME let you key the rate by hand. Universal min/max bounds for the date of injury are applied to every result — automatically.

03 · Round Weeks

Match the WCB rate sheet without retyping.

WCB rate sheets round weeks down — to a tenth or to a whole. Click either pill and every period in the builder snaps to that precision.

Total award, fee, net, the equation card, and the fee-app prefill all use the rounded number. Click again to turn it off and go back to exact weeks.

04 · + Add Period

Stack as many periods as the case calls for.

Each new period's start date is auto-set to the previous period's end date so you can build a chain without retyping. Designation defaults to TT; flip it per row as the case shifts.

Delete a period from its own × button. Reorder by deleting and re-adding — there's no drag-shuffle yet, by design.

05 · CCP Amount + "CCP same"

The ⅓ fee on the CCP, computed for you.

Enter the CCP amount and the calculator adds a ÷ 3 fee on top of the 15% on the moving award. The total fee and net update live.

Hit CCP same and the field auto-fills with the rate from your last period — for when the CCP just continues the same weekly amount you've been building.

06 · Prior Payments

Anything the carrier already paid comes off first.

Type the prior-payment total here. It's subtracted from the gross award before the 15% attorney fee — so prior payments never get fee'd twice.

Combined with the per-period + REIMB ER toggle on each row, the net + fee + reimbursements + prior payments will always reconcile to the gross award.
