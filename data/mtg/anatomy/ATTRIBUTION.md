## Modifications made for use on thecompdesk.com

- Right-side bones are mirrored at runtime in Three.js to produce a full
  bilateral skeleton (the source model only contains midline + right-side
  bones, with the convention that the viewer mirrors the right side).
- No mesh geometry has been altered. Only the runtime presentation
  (camera, lighting, hover/click materials, region mapping) is custom.
