# SEO Audit — thecompdesk.com

**Audit date:** 2026-05-08
**Domain:** https://thecompdesk.com (canonical, no www)
**Auditor:** Website Team
**Scope:** All public HTML pages plus auth/private noindex check
**Comparison baseline:** April 5, 2026 audit (titles 100%, meta desc 82%, OG 53%, Twitter 0%, canonical 53%, JSON-LD 6%, robots 0%)

---

## Executive Summary

The April 6 OG/Twitter overhaul largely held. Site-wide title and meta description coverage is now effectively 100% for public pages. OG and Twitter Card coverage is ~95%. JSON-LD coverage jumped from 6% to ~95%. Canonical coverage is ~95%.

Three structural problems remain:

1. **CRITICAL — www vs non-www mismatch.** Four pages (`index.html`, `for-attorneys.html`, `attorneys.html`, `extension-privacy.html`) declare canonical and og:url as `https://www.thecompdesk.com/...` while the rest of the site, sitemap.xml, and the actual served domain use the bare apex `https://thecompdesk.com`. This splits link equity and produces inconsistent canonical signals on the highest-priority pages, including the homepage.
2. **HIGH — sitemap is stale.** Eleven publicly-indexable pages built since April 6 are missing from `sitemap.xml`, and one URL (`/privacy.html`) points to a file that no longer exists at that path.
3. **HIGH — `og:site_name` is missing site-wide.** Zero pages include this property. Easy global fix.

Below is the per-page inventory.
