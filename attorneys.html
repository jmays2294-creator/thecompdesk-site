Who built this

Built by a workers' comp attorney. For workers' comp attorneys.

I'm Joel Mays, a NYS Workers' Compensation attorney at Shulman & Hill, PLLC. The Comp Desk started where the need was most obvious — in the hearing room. AWW calculations done on scrap paper. SLU percentages pulled from memory. Fee application equations scribbled by hand. So I built the tools the room was missing.

Every calculator in The Comp Desk Pro was built because I needed it for my own cases — and refined because the hearings I was preparing for couldn't tolerate "close enough."
