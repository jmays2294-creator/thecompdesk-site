Frequently asked

The questions attorneys ask before they switch.

Will this replace my existing Litify workflow?

No. The Comp Desk Pro lives alongside Litify, not instead of it. Firm tier exports calculator results, periods, fee equations, and case data to Litify so the work you do here flows into the system of record. Many attorneys keep both open during a hearing.

How is this different from doing the math in Excel?

Three things: cited results (every output shows the WCL section it implements), correctness updates (statutory caps, IME guideline revisions, and rate-table changes are maintained in one place), and integration (the calculator output flows directly into the OC-400.1 fee application and the Pro Workspace case file). Excel doesn't update when the Board changes a rule.

Is the Firm tier worth $99/month over three Pro seats?

If you have three or more attorneys, yes — Firm is the same per-seat math as three Pro seats but includes Litify/Salesforce integration, case data export, unlimited seats, and priority support. The integration is what most firms switch for.

Can I trust the SLU and LWEC math in a hearing?

Yes. Every calculator is built and maintained by Joel Mays, Esq. — a practicing NYS WC attorney at Shulman & Hill — and used in real cases daily before it ships to the rest of the platform. Every result cites the WCL section, IME guideline, or Board rule behind it so you can defend the number on the record.

What does the 14-day free trial include?

Everything in Pro: all 12 calculators, the Pro Workspace with case save/sync, OC-400.1 fee application generation, and iOS + web access. No credit card required to start. Cancel inside the trial and you pay nothing.

What about claimants? Can I send them here?

Yes. The free calculators (AWW, Rate Lookup, plus all 7 on the public calculator index) are free for anyone. Workers can also use Comp Buddy on iOS to find a doctor, get IME reminders, and learn their rights. The free worker-side experience is at /worker.
